# Conversation pairs

Provide `Conversation.csv` with `question` and `answer` columns and at least 10 distinct nonempty pairs. No original corpus or pretrained weights were published in this repository, so their provenance cannot be established here. Use a corpus you have permission to use. Training saves its tokenizer together with its weights; do not mix unrelated assets. Ten pairs is only a smoke-test minimum, not enough for a useful chatbot.
